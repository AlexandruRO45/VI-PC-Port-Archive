# Code of conduct

Leonida is built in the open by a very large number of people and their coding agents. This page is the short
list of what keeps the issues, pull requests and comments usable for all of them. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
has the technical rules; this one is about how we treat each other and the tracker.

## Expected

- **Be kind.** Disagree with the work, not the person. Most PRs and reports here are volunteer work; thank people,
  give evidence, and assume good faith.
- **Keep the tracker for work.** An issue is a bug report with a commit, a command and the output, a concrete
  request, or a measured finding. Questions, chat and announcements belong on [Discord](https://discord.gg/rexed).
- **One place per thing.** Claims, status and findings go in the coordination issue ("Coordinating who works on
  what"), not in a new issue. Search before opening a PR or an issue; don't open a second one for the same thing.
- **You answer for your agents.** If a coding agent posts or opens PRs for you, its posts are yours: it follows
  this page and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), says that it is AI-assisted, and doesn't flood (one comment per point,
  no repeated pings).

## Not accepted

- Insults, slurs, harassment, sexual remarks, and personal attacks of any kind, including about age or ability.
- Spam, trolling and joke issues or PRs.
- Links to leaked or pirated game files, archive passwords, and requests for where to get the game. See the hard
  rules in AGENTS.md: no game data, nothing about obtaining the game.
- Attachments or links to installers and binaries that aren't the project's own build.
- Ban appeals and other Discord moderation matters: those are for Discord's moderators.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** menu (the `…` on any issue, PR or comment) and choose **Report to repository
admins**. That reaches the maintainers privately, without anyone's address being published here. For something
that breaks GitHub's own rules (threats, doxxing, malware), report it to GitHub as well. For Discord, contact its
moderators there.

## What maintainers may do

Hide or delete comments, close or lock issues and PRs, limit interactions for a while, and block accounts from the
repository.
