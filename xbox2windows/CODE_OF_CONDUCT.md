# Community conduct

Contributors of every experience level are welcome to report a build result, question an interpretation or propose a patch. In issues and reviews, refer to the command, observation or code under discussion. Give others room to correct a result and credit their work when citing it.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may moderate a discussion, remove abusive content or restrict participation to protect contributors.

To raise a concern privately, use the contact email on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/R0Y00). Include a link to the discussion and a short description. GitHub's reporting channel is also available for platform abuse, including concerns involving a maintainer. [Report abuse to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
